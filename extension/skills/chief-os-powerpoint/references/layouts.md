# Slide Layouts

A guide to choosing a layout by purpose, not a fixed template. Spans refer to the 12-column grid in [design-system.md](design-system.md#grid). Adapt a layout when the content needs it, but keep the style tokens. Only L09, L11, L15, L16, L22, L23 and L24 may use text cards, in the [card style](design-system.md#card-style). Images in L05 and L07 take the image card.

| Purpose | Layout | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduce | L01 Title, L02 Section divider | L01: no content title zone, title in 6–9 columns, three lines at most, no agenda or claims. L02: use at major transitions only, no charts or dense text. |
| Assert | L03 Executive statement | The statement is the slide, 8–12 columns. No cards or bullets. |
| Explain | L04 Title and body, L05 Text and image | L04: three to five short points, two bullet levels at most. L05: `5/7`, `7/5`, `4/8` or `8/4`, split by what carries the proof; a decorative image never gets more space than the message. |
| Immerse | L06 Full-page image | Text in a protected zone, with a scrim if needed. Title plus one line. Not for generated images. |
| Showcase | L07 Image gallery | Two to four images with one crop ratio and consistent captions, each with distinct evidence. |
| Emphasise metrics | L08 Large number, L09 Metric row | L08: one dominant number with its unit, period and a one-line interpretation. L09: two to four metrics with identical anatomy and consistent precision, at most one highlighted. |
| Prove with data | L10 Data insight, L20 Table | L10: chart in 8–12 columns, direct annotations, highlight only what the title claims. L20: header row distinct, numbers right-aligned, at most 7 rows and 6 columns, 14 pt or more, split rather than shrink. |
| Compare | L11 Comparison, L12 Before and after | `6/6`, like with like, same depth on both sides, recommendation marked explicitly. L12: current state left, future right, change shown by labels and structure, not colour alone. |
| Sequence | L13 Process, L14 Timeline | L13: three to seven steps, numbered, verb-led, connectors only for real sequence. L14: proportionate time axis, separate milestones from ongoing work, at most four lanes and eight milestone labels. |
| Structure strategy | L15 Three pillars, L16 Four-part framework, L17 Matrix | Peers only. A quadrant needs meaningfully named axes and directionality, at most 12 labelled items, direct labels. |
| Explain systems | L18 Architecture | Full-width diagram, at most three hierarchy levels, few crossing connectors, labelled boundaries, icons never replace labels. |
| Show narrowing | L19 Funnel | Each stage has a value or criterion. Area implies quantity only when proportional; prefer stepped bars for exact comparison. |
| Add voice | L21 Quote | Short, real, attributed quote in 7–10 columns (Georgia allowed). Never manufacture or paraphrase a quote. |
| Recommend | L22 Recommendation and rationale | `4/8` or `5/7`: decision on the narrow side, rationale on the wide. Make the requested decision explicit and separate facts, assumptions and recommendations. |
| Manage risk | L23 Risks and mitigations | Table, `6/6` or risk/impact/mitigation columns. Severity uses a label and icon plus colour. Include owner or next action. |
| Close with action | L24 Call to action | One decision statement plus two to four next steps with owner and timing. No generic "Questions?" slide unless asked. |
| Support detail | L25 Appendix | Clearly marked, denser but never below the type and accessibility minimums. |
