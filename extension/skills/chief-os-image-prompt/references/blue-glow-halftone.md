# Blue-Glow Halftone Concept

Create a conceptual poster in grayscale halftone collage with a single electric-blue glow. Two everyday objects combine into one visual metaphor. Apply the [text-free rules](text-free-rules.md).

## Scene

Express the supplied idea by combining [PRIMARY_SUBJECT] with [SECONDARY_OBJECT]. [INTERACTION]. Both are black-and-white photographic cut-outs of objects, animals, plants, architecture, or nature. Neither is a human or a human body part.

A single vivid electric cobalt-blue (`#2B4BFF`) airbrushed glow radiates softly from [GLOW_PLACEMENT] and dissolves into halftone dots at its edges. Blue is the only colour in the image.

## Visual Treatment

- Gritty halftone and risograph print texture, fine stipple dot grain, slight photocopy noise, high-contrast monochrome with deep blacks and soft silver highlights
- Background: flat off-white or light warm-grey paper with a subtle fine dot-grid texture
- Thin hairline black drafting lines cross the canvas horizontally and vertically, like architectural guides or crop marks, some passing behind the subject
- Small solid squares, mostly cobalt blue and a few black, of varied sizes, float around the subject like pixel fragments, with faint halftone dot-matrix patches
- Composition: [COMPOSITION], with generous negative space, in a minimalist editorial Swiss-design layout. Keep [NEGATIVE_SPACE] empty. The subject is crisp and in focus
- Format: vertical 4:5, unless the caller sets another ratio
- Mood: inspirational, modern, thoughtful; a premium editorial poster

## Placeholder Definitions

### [PRIMARY_SUBJECT] and [SECONDARY_OBJECT]

A main object, and a second, unexpected object that completes the metaphor. Pair two things that do not belong together.

Examples:

- A vintage radio-telescope satellite dish, with a short rising stone staircase
- A chess queen, with a paper aeroplane
- A match, with a lily

### [INTERACTION]

How the two objects connect, in one sentence.

Examples:

- The dish stands on the topmost step, its receiver pointing upward, with faint concentric signal arcs radiating from the tip.
- The aeroplane's folded nose rests in the queen's crown.

### [GLOW_PLACEMENT]

The one place the blue glow sits, such as behind the dish and along the signal arcs.

### [COMPOSITION] and [NEGATIVE_SPACE]

The framing and placement, and the areas to keep empty. Write `none` for the empty areas when the caller names none.

Examples:

- The dish and staircase centred, the staircase rising diagonally from the lower left, the dish angled up toward the upper right
- The upper-left and lower-right corners

## Human Gestures

When the idea needs a human gesture, such as an offering hand, use a non-human stand-in: a white marble statue hand, a jointed wooden mannequin hand, or a glowing wireframe hand.

## Avoid

- A human, a human body part, a crowd, or a selfie
- Full colour, a second colour, warm tones, or a rainbow gradient
- A cluttered or busy scene, a cartoon look, glossy CGI, a blurry subject, a heavy vignette, frames, or borders
- More than one blue glow
