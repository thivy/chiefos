# Blue-Glow Halftone Concept

A conceptual poster in grayscale halftone collage with a single electric-blue glow. Two everyday objects combine into one visual metaphor. Apply the [text-free rules](text-free-rules.md).

## Scene

Express the supplied idea by combining [PRIMARY_SUBJECT] with [SECONDARY_OBJECT]. [INTERACTION]. Both are black-and-white photographic cut-outs of objects, animals, plants, architecture or nature; neither is a human or a body part.

A single vivid electric cobalt-blue (`#2B4BFF`) airbrushed glow radiates softly from [GLOW_PLACEMENT] and dissolves into halftone dots at its edges. Blue is the only colour in the image.

- **[PRIMARY_SUBJECT], [SECONDARY_OBJECT]:** a main object and a second, unexpected one that completes the metaphor, two things that don't belong together (a vintage radio-telescope dish with a short stone staircase; a chess queen with a paper aeroplane; a match with a lily).
- **[INTERACTION]:** how they connect, in one sentence (the aeroplane's folded nose rests in the queen's crown).
- **[GLOW_PLACEMENT]:** the one place the glow sits (behind the dish and along the signal arcs).

## Visual Treatment

- Gritty halftone and risograph texture, fine stipple, slight photocopy noise, high-contrast monochrome with deep blacks and soft silver highlights
- Flat off-white or light warm-grey paper with a subtle fine dot-grid texture
- Thin hairline black drafting lines cross the canvas horizontally and vertically like architectural guides or crop marks, some passing behind the subject
- Small solid squares, mostly cobalt blue and a few black, of varied sizes, float around the subject like pixel fragments, with faint halftone dot-matrix patches
- Composition: [COMPOSITION], in a minimalist editorial Swiss-design layout with generous negative space; keep [NEGATIVE_SPACE] empty (`none` when the caller names none). The subject is crisp and in focus
- Vertical 4:5, unless the caller sets another ratio. Mood: inspirational, modern, thoughtful; a premium editorial poster

## Human Gestures

When the idea needs a human gesture such as an offering hand, use a white marble statue hand, a jointed wooden mannequin hand or a glowing wireframe hand.

## Avoid

- A human, a body part, a crowd or a selfie
- Full colour, a second colour, warm tones or a rainbow gradient
- A cluttered scene, cartoon look, glossy CGI, blurry subject, heavy vignette, frames or borders
- More than one blue glow
