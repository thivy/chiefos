# Luminous Glitch Hologram

An editorial art poster of one glowing, translucent, glass-like subject on a near-black background, interrupted by a digital glitch effect. Apply the [text-free rules](text-free-rules.md).

## Scene

Show a single [SUBJECT] [VIEW_ANGLE] against a deep matte black background with subtle film grain. It is never a person.

Render it as translucent, ethereal glass: [SURFACE_DETAIL], soft inner light, and iridescent holographic edges in pearl white, lavender, cyan and soft pink. Its core glows with warm [CORE_COLOUR] light that fades outward into pale, semi-transparent extremities. [SECONDARY_ELEMENT]

A [GLITCH_STYLE] digital effect interrupts the image: [GLITCH_DESCRIPTION]. The fragments pick up [CORE_COLOUR], violet, magenta, teal and white, with slight chromatic aberration and an RGB channel split along their edges.

- **[SUBJECT], [VIEW_ANGLE]:** one subject with delicate, layered or translucent parts (a peony in three-quarter side view; a jellyfish from below; a lighthouse in profile; a compass from above). Large solid objects and busy scenes work less well. Use a symbol from the text-free rules for a human idea.
- **[SURFACE_DETAIL], [SECONDARY_ELEMENT]:** fine texture (layered petals with veins; faceted crystalline planes) and an optional small companion (a glass-winged butterfly with a faint curved line; soft concentric signal rings), or nothing.
- **[CORE_COLOUR]:** golden amber-yellow, coral orange, rose pink, electric violet, ice blue or emerald.
- **[GLITCH_STYLE]:** pick one. *Pixel-mosaic:* square pixel blocks and small mosaic tiles break from the subject's edges and drift into the dark. *Horizontal streak:* edges smear into long horizontal light streaks and stretched pixel bars like a corrupted video frame, with a few thin vertical beams.

## Visual Treatment

- Vertical 4:5, unless the caller sets another ratio
- The subject fills about [SUBJECT_SCALE] of the frame, placed [PLACEMENT], [GROUNDING] (for example a stem curving out of frame, or floating with a faint reflection), with generous dark negative space. [OPTIONAL_LINE_ART]: thin white hairline rectangular frames like selection boxes outline the subject and a few sections, with no labels; or nothing
- Mood: [MOOD] (quiet and hopeful, confident and optimistic, mysterious and introspective)
- High-end digital art: [REAL_WORLD_STYLE] (a non-portrait genre: macro botanical, wildlife, underwater, product, architectural or astrophotography) blended with generative glitch art, cinematic low-key lighting, sharp detail in the centre, soft bloom glow

## Avoid

- More than one subject, an opaque matte surface, a flat cartoon look, a low-resolution or blurry subject
- A bright, white or cluttered background, oversaturated neon, frames or borders, labels in the hairline frames
- A person or anything mistakable for one. If a figure is truly needed, make it obviously artificial: a faceless translucent glass mannequin or a figure of glowing particles and wireframe lines, with no facial features or skin texture
