# Luminous Glitch Hologram

Create an editorial art poster of one glowing, translucent, glass-like subject on a near-black background, interrupted by a digital glitch effect. Apply the [text-free rules](text-free-rules.md).

## Scene

Show a single [SUBJECT] [VIEW_ANGLE] against a deep matte black background with subtle film grain. It is never a person.

Render the subject as translucent, ethereal glass: [SURFACE_DETAIL], soft inner light, and iridescent holographic edges shimmering in pearl white, lavender, cyan, and soft pink. Its core glows with warm [CORE_COLOUR] light that fades outward into pale, semi-transparent extremities. [SECONDARY_ELEMENT]

A [GLITCH_STYLE] digital effect interrupts the image: [GLITCH_DESCRIPTION]. The fragments pick up [CORE_COLOUR], violet, magenta, teal, and white, with slight chromatic aberration and an RGB channel split along their edges.

## Visual Treatment

- Format: vertical 4:5, unless the caller sets another ratio
- Composition: the subject fills about [SUBJECT_SCALE] of the frame, placed [PLACEMENT], [GROUNDING], with generous dark negative space around the edges. [OPTIONAL_LINE_ART]
- Mood: [MOOD]
- High-end digital art, [REAL_WORLD_STYLE] blended with generative glitch art, cinematic low-key lighting, sharp detail in the centre, soft bloom glow

## Placeholder Definitions

### [SUBJECT] and [VIEW_ANGLE]

One subject with delicate, layered, or translucent parts, and the angle on it. Large solid objects and busy scenes work less well. Use a symbol from the text-free rules for a human idea.

Examples:

- A peony in full bloom, in three-quarter side view
- A jellyfish, from slightly below
- A lighthouse, in profile
- A compass, from above

### [SURFACE_DETAIL] and [SECONDARY_ELEMENT]

The subject's fine texture, and an optional small companion, or nothing.

Examples:

- Layered petals with fine visible veins
- Faceted crystalline planes
- A translucent glass-winged butterfly hovers above left, joined by a faint thin curved line.
- Soft concentric signal rings radiate from the core.

### [CORE_COLOUR]

The warm centre colour, such as golden amber-yellow, coral orange, rose pink, electric violet, ice blue, or emerald.

### [GLITCH_STYLE] and [GLITCH_DESCRIPTION]

Pick one style, with its description.

- **Pixel-mosaic:** scattered square pixel blocks and small rectangular mosaic tiles break away from the subject's edges, partly dissolving it into blocky digital fragments that drift outward into the dark.
- **Horizontal streak:** the subject's edges smear into long horizontal light streaks and stretched pixel bars, like a corrupted video frame, with a few thin vertical light beams crossing the composition.

### [SUBJECT_SCALE], [PLACEMENT], and [GROUNDING]

How much of the frame the subject fills, where it sits, and how it is anchored.

Examples:

- Two-thirds of the frame, slightly right of centre, with a thin elegant stem curving down and out of frame
- Half of the frame, centred, floating with a faint reflection below
- Three-quarters of the frame, in the lower left, rising from darkness at the bottom edge

### [OPTIONAL_LINE_ART]

Fine-line detail, or nothing. Thin white hairline rectangular frames, like digital selection boxes, outline the subject and a few small sections. They carry no labels.

### [MOOD] and [REAL_WORLD_STYLE]

The feeling in a few words, such as quiet and hopeful, confident and optimistic, or mysterious and introspective. Then a non-portrait photography genre to blend with: macro botanical, wildlife, underwater, product, architectural, or astrophotography.

## Avoid

- More than one subject, an opaque matte surface, a flat cartoon look, or a low-resolution or blurry subject
- A bright, white, or cluttered background, oversaturated neon, or frames and borders
- Labels inside the hairline frames
- A person, or any form that could be mistaken for one. If a figure is truly needed, make it obviously artificial, such as a faceless translucent glass mannequin or a figure of glowing particles and wireframe lines, with no facial features and no skin texture
