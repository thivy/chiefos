# Text-Free Rules

Shared rules for the styles that render no text and no real-looking people: Prism Grain Silhouette, Spectrum Grain Collage, Blue-Glow Halftone Concept and Luminous Glitch Hologram. Each of those states only what differs. This file is not a style.

## Text

- Render no text: no letters, words, numbers, captions, labels, quotations, logos, watermarks, signatures, file names or symbols that read as writing. The whitelist is empty.
- If the caller supplies fields to render, don't use a text-free style; say so and name Everyday Doodle and Scientific Editorial. If the caller needs words or numbers on the artwork, they add them afterwards, so keep the subject off the area they name.

## People

- No real-looking human: no photorealistic person, face, eyes, lips, skin, hair detail, hands or body. Describe a figure by silhouette, clothing and pose only, never face, age, skin or ethnicity. Never use `photorealistic`, `lifelike` or `portrait` for a figure.
- For a human idea use one stand-in the style allows (faceless stylised silhouette, classical marble statue, faceless mannequin, wooden manikin, chrome robot, wireframe or crystalline figure, flat graphic silhouette), or a symbol: leadership is a lighthouse, compass or north star; teamwork is birds in formation, interlocking rings or a constellation; growth is a blooming flower or seedling; creativity is a glass light bulb, hummingbird or prism; helping is two branches meeting or a bridge of light; thinking is a chess knight, owl or branching coral.

## Composition

One hero subject and one idea in generous negative space, with no second focal point. Keep the style's palette locked; an extra colour breaks the look.

## Closing Lines

Models drift towards text and realistic people, so repeat both sentences at the start and the end of every composed prompt, marked as instructions for the image model, never as image text:

- `Strictly no real-looking humans: no photorealistic people, no realistic faces, eyes, skin texture, or hair detail.`
- `Strictly no text, no letters, no words, no numbers, no typography, no captions, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures.`
