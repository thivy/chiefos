# Voxel Storyworld

Build a small, tactile world entirely from cubes, where a surprising change of scale makes one idea immediately understandable.

## Scene

Place one dominant [VOXEL_SUBJECT] on a shallow, rectilinear base against a pale mineral-gray studio background. Construct the entire scene from solid, grid-aligned voxels, including terrain, water, foliage, props, and any characters. The subject occupies roughly two-thirds of the built composition and remains recognizable by its silhouette.

Use [SPATIAL_STORY] to turn the supplied meaning into one readable arrangement, interaction, or frozen moment. Add [PURPOSEFUL_DETAILS] to establish scale and reveal how the place works. Prefer an environment shaped by the subject, an unexpected scale relationship, or a useful cutaway over a generic block-built landscape.

Make the scene feel discovered rather than assembled for display: a path leads somewhere, a bridge connects something, an opening reveals a useful interior. Every addition belongs to the same physical world and supports the supplied idea without introducing new factual claims. The image must work without explanatory text.

## Visual Treatment

- True three-dimensional voxel sculpture, not a pixel-art filter, flat sprite, or triangular low-poly illustration
- Crisp, unbeveled cube faces and deliberate stair-step contours; translate curves into stepped geometry rather than smoothing them
- One consistent voxel grid, with large clustered masses and a few medium-scale details; the smallest feature is one whole voxel, never a scatter of tiny pixel noise
- A slightly elevated, three-quarter orthographic view showing the top and two sides; no wide-angle distortion or dramatic perspective
- An asymmetrical composition with one strong silhouette, a clear foreground approach, and generous negative space; keep the entire subject and base inside the frame
- Matte, densely pigmented model-making blocks with clean surfaces; material identity comes from color, massing, and stepped structure rather than photographic textures
- Pale mineral gray and chalk white balanced by deep petrol and coral; reserve a small marigold accent for the important action or destination, respecting supplied brand colors when required
- A broad, soft key light from the upper left and restrained cool fill; distinct light, middle, and shaded faces make each block readable
- Gentle ambient occlusion at joints and contact points, with one coherent soft cast shadow anchoring the base; never crush the shaded faces into black
- Full-depth sharpness, clear edges, and restrained surface detail; miniature scale comes from construction and proportion, not blurred surroundings
- Suggest action through a raised gate, an almost-completed connection, a transferred object, or an illuminated destination; no motion blur or decorative speed lines
- For multiple items, use one distinct vignette per item in the supplied order, with a shared grid scale, camera, palette, and lighting; vary silhouettes and actions while keeping clear space between scenes

## Placeholder Definitions

### [VOXEL_SUBJECT]

The main object, structure, place, or system. Describe its recognizable outline, stepped construction, dominant colors, orientation, scale, and placement. Keep its defining features visible even when reinterpreting it as a miniature environment.

Examples:

- An open sea-green book whose thick ivory page stacks form terraced slopes on either side of a deep central crease
- A compact coral sluice house on a cutaway of chalk-colored ground, with a squared teal water channel entering from the left

### [SPATIAL_STORY]

Choose one device that makes the supplied idea visible: a contrast of scale, a connected route, an interaction, a revealing cutaway, or a change of state. Describe the physical relationship precisely. Do not force machinery, a journey, or a before-and-after sequence onto content that does not call for it.

**Template**

Use [STRUCTURAL_FEATURE] as [STORY_ROLE], with [VISIBLE_ACTION_OR_RELATIONSHIP] making [SUPPLIED_IDEA] legible.

Examples:

- Let the open book's pages become a valley crossed by one coral bridge, turning accumulated knowledge into a usable connection.
- Expose the sluice house's raised gate and continuous water channel, making the release of a blockage visible from inlet to outlet.

### [PURPOSEFUL_DETAILS]

Choose two to four small details that establish scale, use, or cause and effect. Connect them to an actual surface, route, or operation. They should reward closer viewing without becoming competing subjects.

**Template**

Place [SCALE_CUE] beside [SUBJECT_FEATURE], and add [CONNECTED_DETAIL] to show [USE_OR_CHANGE].

Examples:

- Add one-voxel-wide handrails along the book's bridge and a small reading alcove inset into the far page bank where the crossing ends.
- Fix a cuboid maintenance ladder to the sluice wall and show three shallow, stepped cascades immediately downstream of the open gate.

Characters are optional, never automatic decoration. Include them only when the supplied content calls for people; use simple cube-built figures with readable poses and visible contact, without detailed faces or invented identities.

Render only the caller's exact text whitelist. Place permitted labels in reserved background space, clear of the geometry, using the supplied typography. If no rendered text is supplied, keep the image text-free. Never display placeholders or prompt instructions.

## Avoid

- Random piles of cubes, noisy microvoxels, flat pixel art, pixelated photographs, or triangular meshes
- Generic game-world screenshots, recognizable game assets, branded toy-brick styling, studs, or interface overlays
- Smooth spheres, cylinders, rounded edges, beveled cubes, sculpted anatomy, or non-voxel props
- Unexplained floating blocks, unsupported structures, disconnected paths, broken stairs, or objects without visible contact
- Unrelated scenery, duplicated focal subjects, excessive miniature props, or evenly tiled compositions
- Glossy plastic, metallic glare, photographic surface textures, heavy outlines, or distressed block edges
- Neon glow, colored fog, atmospheric particles, dramatic bloom, strong depth-of-field blur, or crushed shadows
- Invented data, unrequested characters, decorative captions, speech bubbles, logos, watermarks, or illegible lettering
