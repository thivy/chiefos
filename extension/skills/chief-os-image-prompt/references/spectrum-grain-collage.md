# Spectrum Grain Collage

A grainy risograph and photocopy collage: a monochrome photo cut-out of one non-human subject with violet, burnt-orange and lime-green accents on warm cream paper. Apply the [text-free rules](text-free-rules.md).

## Scene

Place [MAIN_SUBJECT] [SUBJECT_POSE_OR_ACTION] at [SUBJECT_POSITION], as a high-contrast black-and-white vintage photograph cut-out with heavy film grain and photocopy texture. The subject is an animal, object, landscape, architecture or clearly non-real figure, never a real-looking human.

Layer [SECONDARY_OBJECTS] behind or beside it in the same grainy monochrome. When the idea suits it, add [RETRO_TECH_OBJECT] whose screen glows with a violet-to-orange gradient (write `none` otherwise).

Behind the subject sits a large textured [BACKDROP_SHAPE] in vivid electric violet (`#8A2BE2`), like a sun or halo, with a smaller [ACCENT_SHAPE] in burnt orange (`#FF4500`). Both have an airbrushed, speckled spray-paint texture and soft dissolving edges.

- **[MAIN_SUBJECT], [SUBJECT_POSE_OR_ACTION]:** the hero and what it does, never a real-looking person (a galloping horse with its mane flying; a wooden artist's manikin raising a vintage satellite dish; a snow-capped peak).
- **[SECONDARY_OBJECTS]:** two supporting objects that carry the idea (stacked stone steps and clouds). **[RETRO_TECH_OBJECT]:** a CRT monitor, antenna TV or cassette player.
- **[BACKDROP_SHAPE], [ACCENT_SHAPE], [OPTIONAL_ACCENT]:** a circle, sphere or blurred disc; a small planet, glow or half circle; one extra touch such as a lime four-point sparkle or signal-wave arcs, or `none`.

## Visual Treatment

- Vertical 4:5, unless the caller sets another ratio
- Selective colour: photographic elements stay black and white; only [COLOUR_POP_AREAS] carry violet, orange and lime
- Small floating squares in lime green (`#9AFF3C`), hot orange-red and violet, some with soft gradient fills and slight transparency, overlapping the subject like glitch pixels
- One thin black elliptical orbit line looping around the subject, plus [OPTIONAL_ACCENT]
- Spray-dust particles and grainy colour bursts trail from the subject [MOTION_DIRECTION] (`none, static` is allowed)
- Warm off-white cream paper (`#F1EADF`) with visible grain, faint speckle and clean negative space
- Mood: [MOOD] (contemplative and quiet, dynamic and unstoppable, triumphant and rising), creative and bold, contemporary meets retro; risograph, Y2K-meets-90s zine, halftone grain, analogue noise

## Human Stand-Ins

When the idea needs a person, use a classical marble or plaster statue, a faceless store mannequin, a wooden artist's manikin, a retro chrome or tin robot, a flat silhouette filled with halftone grain, or an animal that carries the same meaning (horse, wolf, eagle, owl, fox).

## Avoid

- Real-looking people, portraits or crowds
- Full-colour photography, glossy 3D, clean vector flatness, a cluttered background
- Colours outside violet, burnt orange and lime
