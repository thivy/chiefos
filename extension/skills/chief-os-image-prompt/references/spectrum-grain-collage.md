# Spectrum Grain Collage

Create a grainy risograph and photocopy collage: a monochrome photo cut-out of one non-human subject, with violet, burnt-orange, and lime-green accents on warm cream paper. Apply the [text-free rules](text-free-rules.md).

## Scene

Place [MAIN_SUBJECT] [SUBJECT_POSE_OR_ACTION] at [SUBJECT_POSITION], as a high-contrast black-and-white vintage photograph cut-out with heavy film grain and photocopy texture. The subject is an animal, an object, a landscape, architecture, or a clearly non-real figure, and never a real-looking human.

Layer [SECONDARY_OBJECTS] behind or beside it, in the same grainy monochrome. Add [RETRO_TECH_OBJECT] whose screen glows with a violet-to-orange gradient, when the idea suits it.

Behind the subject sits a large textured [BACKDROP_SHAPE] in vivid electric violet (`#8A2BE2`), like a sun or halo, with a smaller [ACCENT_SHAPE] in burnt orange (`#FF4500`). Both have an airbrushed, speckled spray-paint texture and soft dissolving edges.

## Visual Treatment

- Format: vertical 4:5, unless the caller sets another ratio
- Selective colour: photographic elements stay black and white. Only [COLOUR_POP_AREAS] carry violet, orange, and lime
- Small floating squares in lime green (`#9AFF3C`), hot orange-red, and violet, some with soft gradient fills and slight transparency, overlapping the subject like glitch pixels
- One thin black elliptical orbit line looping around the subject, plus [OPTIONAL_ACCENT]
- Spray-dust particles and grainy colour bursts trail from the subject [MOTION_DIRECTION]
- Background: warm off-white cream paper (`#F1EADF`) with visible paper grain, faint speckle noise, and clean negative space around the composition
- Mood: [MOOD], creative and bold, contemporary meets retro; risograph print, Y2K-meets-90s zine, halftone grain, analogue noise

## Placeholder Definitions

### [MAIN_SUBJECT] and [SUBJECT_POSE_OR_ACTION]

The hero, and what it is doing. Never a real-looking person.

Examples:

- A galloping horse, mid-gallop with its mane flying
- A wooden artist's posing manikin, standing tall with one arm raised and holding a vintage satellite dish skyward
- A snow-capped mountain peak, balanced on a summit

### [SUBJECT_POSITION]

Examples: centred and slightly low; left of centre; the lower third.

### [SECONDARY_OBJECTS] and [RETRO_TECH_OBJECT]

Two supporting objects that carry the idea, and an optional vintage device. Write `none` for the device when the idea does not need one.

Examples:

- Two stacked stone steps and drifting clouds
- A giant pencil and orange hibiscus flowers
- A CRT monitor, an antenna TV set, or a cassette player

### [BACKDROP_SHAPE], [ACCENT_SHAPE], and [OPTIONAL_ACCENT]

The violet shape, the smaller orange shape, and one extra touch or `none`.

Examples:

- A circle, a sphere, or a soft blurred disc
- A small planet, a soft glow, or a half circle
- A lime four-point sparkle star, or concentric signal-wave arcs

### [COLOUR_POP_AREAS] and [MOTION_DIRECTION]

Which parts take colour, and where the energy flows.

Examples:

- The TV screen and the dust trail; upward
- The flower petals; behind the subject to the left
- The mountain shadows and the signal waves; none, static

### [MOOD]

The feeling in a few words, such as contemplative and quiet, dynamic and unstoppable, or triumphant and rising.

## Human Stand-Ins

When the idea needs a person, use a classical marble or plaster statue, a faceless store mannequin, a wooden artist's posing manikin, a retro chrome or tin robot, a solid flat silhouette filled with halftone grain, or an animal that carries the same meaning, such as a horse, wolf, eagle, owl, or fox.

## Avoid

- Real-looking people, portraits, or crowds
- Full-colour photography, a glossy 3D render, clean vector flatness, or a cluttered background
- Colours outside violet, burnt orange, and lime
