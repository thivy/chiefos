# Prism Grain Silhouette

A minimalist, dreamlike, inspirational poster illustration: mostly greyscale and grainy, with one prism-rainbow glow, one small stylised figure and one oversized symbolic element. Apply the [text-free rules](text-free-rules.md).

## Scene

Place one small [FIGURE] [ACTION_OR_POSE] at [FIGURE_POSITION]. The figure is a stylised illustrated silhouette: faceless, smooth featureless head, simplified graphic anatomy, soft grainy shading, like a paper cut-out or matte sculpted figurine. It never looks like a real or photographed person.

Dominating the composition is [HERO_ELEMENT], [SCALE] in scale, [HERO_BEHAVIOUR], conveying [THEME_OR_EMOTION]. The scale contrast between figure and hero creates the feeling.

A single prismatic rainbow light refraction glows across [RAINBOW_PLACEMENT], like light through a crystal prism. [GROUNDING].

- **[FIGURE]:** described by silhouette and clothing only (a figure in a long flowing coat; a seated painter silhouette in a cap).
- **[ACTION_OR_POSE], [FIGURE_POSITION]:** what it does and where it sits, usually low to leave room above (gazing upward, lower centre; reaching to the sky on a pillar).
- **[HERO_ELEMENT]:** one oversized symbolic object, for example a towering translucent poppy leaning over the figure, a giant paper aeroplane gliding upward, or signal-wave rings rising from the figure's hand.
- **[THEME_OR_EMOTION]:** the idea in two or three words (imagination, taking the leap, steady progress).
- **[RAINBOW_PLACEMENT]:** the one place the prism light lands (the lower petals; the folded wing crease). Keep the rest greyscale.
- **[GROUNDING]:** a tall lavender-grey plinth, a soft pale reflective floor, or floating with no ground.

## Visual Treatment

- Vertical 4:5 with generous negative space, unless the caller sets another ratio
- Greyscale of soft whites, warm greys and deep charcoal; the only colour is the prism glow (orange, coral, magenta, violet, a little blue) plus lavender-tinted shadows
- Fine grain and stipple, like risograph or noisy airbrush; soft dissolving edges
- Clean off-white to pale grey gradient background, soft holographic light leaks and lens flares from the edges, very thin white curved arc and orbit lines; two tiny flat accents, one violet dot and one violet square, in empty space
- Mood: calm, contemplative, airy, poetic; soft diffused high-key light; stylised digital illustration with a grainy print finish, never photography

## Avoid

- More than one figure or hero element, or a busy background
- Rainbow colour outside the one glow, or any full-colour scene
- A dark background, harsh shadows, cartoon outlines, frames or borders
- A visible face, eyes, realistic skin or hair on the figure; if one appears, make it flatter and more featureless
