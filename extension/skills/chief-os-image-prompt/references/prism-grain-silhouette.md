# Prism Grain Silhouette

Create a minimalist, dreamlike, inspirational poster illustration: mostly greyscale and grainy, with one prism-rainbow glow, one small stylised figure, and one oversized symbolic element. Apply the [text-free rules](text-free-rules.md).

## Scene

Place one small [FIGURE] [ACTION_OR_POSE] at [FIGURE_POSITION]. The figure is a stylised illustrated silhouette: faceless, with a smooth featureless head, simplified graphic anatomy, and soft grainy shading, like a paper cut-out or a matte sculpted figurine. It never looks like a real or photographed person.

Dominating the composition is [HERO_ELEMENT] at [SCALE] scale, [HERO_BEHAVIOUR], conveying [THEME_OR_EMOTION]. The scale contrast between the figure and the hero element creates the feeling.

A single prismatic rainbow light refraction glows across [RAINBOW_PLACEMENT], like light passing through a crystal prism. [GROUNDING].

## Visual Treatment

- Format: vertical 4:5 with generous negative space, unless the caller sets another ratio
- Greyscale palette of soft whites, warm greys, and deep charcoal; the only colour is the prism glow in orange, coral, magenta, violet, and a little blue, plus lavender-tinted shadows
- Fine grain and stipple shading, like a risograph print or airbrush with noise; soft, dissolving edges
- Background: clean off-white to pale grey gradient, soft holographic light leaks and lens flares bleeding in from the edges, and very thin white curved arc and orbit lines across the scene
- Two tiny flat accents, one violet dot and one violet square, floating in empty space
- Mood: calm, contemplative, airy, poetic; soft diffused high-key light; a stylised digital illustration with a grainy print finish, never photography

## Placeholder Definitions

### [FIGURE]

One small figure, described by silhouette and clothing only.

Examples:

- A figure in a long flowing coat
- A small child-shaped silhouette in a loose tunic
- A seated painter silhouette in a cap

### [ACTION_OR_POSE] and [FIGURE_POSITION]

What the figure does, and where it sits in the frame, usually low so the poster keeps room above.

Examples:

- Gazing upward, in the lower centre
- Reaching one hand to the sky, on top of a pillar in the lower centre
- Stepping forward, in the bottom-left corner

### [HERO_ELEMENT], [SCALE], and [HERO_BEHAVIOUR]

One oversized symbolic object, its size against the figure, and its motion or state.

**Template**

[HERO_ELEMENT], [SCALE] in scale, [HERO_BEHAVIOUR].

Examples:

- A towering translucent poppy on a long slender stem, monumental in scale, gently leaning over the figure
- A giant paper aeroplane trailing loose paper fragments, oversized in scale, gliding diagonally upward
- Concentric translucent signal-wave rings, monumental in scale, growing larger as they rise from the figure's hand

### [THEME_OR_EMOTION]

The idea in two or three words, such as imagination, personal growth, taking the leap, or steady progress.

### [RAINBOW_PLACEMENT]

The one place the prism light lands. Keep the rainbow to one area, so that the rest stays greyscale.

Examples:

- The lower petals and one leaf
- The folded wing crease
- The innermost signal rings nearest the hand

### [GROUNDING]

What holds the figure, or that it floats.

Examples:

- The figure stands on a tall lavender-grey plinth.
- The figure stands on a soft pale reflective floor.
- The figure floats with no ground.

## Avoid

- More than one figure, more than one hero element, or a busy background
- Rainbow colour outside the one glow area, or any full-colour scene
- A dark background, harsh shadows, cartoon outlines, frames, or borders
- A visible face, eyes, realistic skin, or realistic hair on the figure; if one appears, make the figure flatter and more featureless
